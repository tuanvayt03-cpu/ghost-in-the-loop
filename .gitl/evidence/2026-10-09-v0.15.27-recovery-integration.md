2. Verified Send failure requires SEND_TIMEOUT plus stable user and assistant count/text, non-generating host, owned attempt.
3. An uncertain attempt during active host generation or changing assistant text is frozen; no resend, no automatic HUMAN escalation.
4. Transient gate is automatically cleared only on positive new user/assistant progress, NOT on Stop/BUSY alone.
5. Explicit operator Resume on transient WEB_SEND_UNCERTAIN while host is generating clears that gate and adopts current turn without Send.
6. Hard AUTH_ERROR and unrelated gates remain guarded.
7. Ghost-only PAUSED status can coexist with ChatGPT active Stop; panel distinguishes those states.
8. Existing task-preserving tab title, ChatGPT-only deployment, no unrelated draft overwrite and immutable loader contracts remain unchanged.
