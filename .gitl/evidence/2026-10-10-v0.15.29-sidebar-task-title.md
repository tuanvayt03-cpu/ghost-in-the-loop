## Root cause
`sidebarTaskName()` scanned all anchors with a matching resolved pathname. Same-page accessibility skip links (`href="#main"`, label `Chuyển đến nội dung`) resolved to the same chat pathname and won over the actual conversation link. Even for an authentic conversation anchor, tooltip/`aria-label` took precedence over visible text. During operator locks, this poisoned the document title with `🔴 Chuyển đến nội dung`.

## Correction
- Require an explicit same-origin conversation `/c/<id>` href with matching pathname and no hash.
- Prefer visible sidebar conversation text over attributes, and reject navigation/placeholder labels.
- Prevent stale generic titles from entering cached task titles; restore the real title when a gate clears.
- Display one `🔴` for HUMAN gates and retain existing `🟠` for relay; no verbose HUMAN/RELAY prefix.
