- Reproduction: Ghost+ v0.15.32 could stage a WATCHDOG RECOVERY STATUS PROBE while the panel returned to IDLE / Stopped instead of advancing the recovery turn.

## Root causes
1. Stale Ghost Play control after Stop:
   - watchdog recover() captured both Stop and Play before clicking Stop;
   - core Stop re-renders #gitl9 with innerHTML;
   - the captured Play node became detached, so clicking the stale reference could leave the probe staged while Ghost remained stopped.
2. Receipt mismatch after successful Send:
   - current ChatGPT rendering can collapse/remove line-boundary whitespace from a submitted multiline prompt;
   - the user turn existed and Send occurred exactly once, but chatgptSubmissionObserved required normalized text equality that still preserved a whitespace boundary;
   - core therefore remained in Staging initial task instead of committing round 1.

## Repair
- Re-acquire the live Play control only after Stop, probe staging, and final host-control safety confirmation.
- If the fresh Play control is missing or click throws before dispatch, clear only the exact Ghost-managed staged probe and fail closed.
- Receipt matching now accepts whitespace-only rendering drift only when:
  - a valid pre-submit snapshot exists;
  - user-turn count has genuinely increased;
  - all non-whitespace prompt characters remain identical.
- No fuzzy text matching was introduced. A substantive text change remains rejected.
- Runtime bumped to 0.15.33.
- Tampermonkey loader bumped to 9.0.0-alpha.2+ghostplus.15.33.
